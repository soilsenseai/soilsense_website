Capture your soil photo

Use your phone's rear camera or choose an existing image. For a useful photo, fill the frame with soil, use good lighting, and avoid including too much scenery.

Choose “Start Live Camera” or “Take Photo / Choose Image” to begin.

Before you begin

Live camera access usually requires this website to be opened over HTTPS. If you opened the HTML file directly from your phone's file manager and live camera does not work, use “Take Photo / Choose Image” or host the site over HTTPS. The photo stays on your device in this prototype; it is not uploaded to a server, and AI analysis is not connected yet.
