What is Soil Sense?

SoilSense is an AI-powered agricultural advisory platform designed to help farmers and gardeners analyze soil, assess soil fertility, discover suitable crops and plants, and understand weather-related risks.

About Our Project

Our project, AI-Based Soil Fertility Detection, uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help determine the fertility of soil. Soil fertility is essential for healthy plant growth and better crop production. Our system analyzes important soil parameters, such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, moisture, and other available measurements, to estimate soil fertility. By using AI technology, we aim to provide quick, informative, and data-driven results that help users understand their soil and make better decisions for agriculture.

Core Pillars of SoilSense

Our Mission

Our mission is to combine Artificial Intelligence with agricultural science to make soil fertility assessment easier, smarter, and more accessible. We aim to develop a user-friendly system that helps students, farmers, gardeners, and agricultural researchers understand soil conditions through data-driven analysis.

How Will It Work?

Our AI-based system works by analyzing soil data entered by the user or collected through soil-testing sensors. The system processes parameters such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, and moisture. An AI model trained on soil data identifies patterns to estimate fertility levels instantly.

Why Soil Fertility?

Soil fertility refers to the ability of soil to provide essential nutrients and suitable conditions for plant growth. Fertile soil contains key nutrients, organic matter, and moisture. Understanding fertility helps farmers select crops, manage fertilizers, and improve yields.

User Guide

HOW TO USE SOILSENSE?

Getting started with SoilSense is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps to analyze your soil in real time using AI technology:

1 Step 1: Position Your Camera
  • Bring your device (phone, laptop, or webcam) close to the soil sample you wish to test.
  • Ensure adequate natural lighting or turn on your flash/light so the soil texture, color, and moisture level are clearly visible.
2 Step 2: Enable Camera Access
  • Click the Start Camera button on the SoilSense portal.
  • When prompted by your browser, click "Allow" to give SoilSense permission to access your camera feed.
3 Step 3: Capture Soil Image
  • On the camera page, use Take Photo to capture the soil, or choose a photo from your phone gallery. Check the preview before continuing.
4 Step 4: AI Analysis + Instant Results
  • The current prototype captures and previews a soil image. AI predictions will be enabled after the trained model is connected to the website backend.
  • Until the backend is connected, do not treat the photo as a soil test or as a measurement of pH, nutrients, or fertility.
